=== PARA PH FARE MATRIX & RULES ===
- Jeepney Base Fare: ₱13.00 for the first 4 kilometers.
- Jeepney Per KM: ₱2.50 for every kilometer after the first 4km.
- LRT-1 Base Fare: ₱15.00.
- MRT-3 Base Fare: ₱13.00.
- Senior Citizens and PWDs are entitled to a 20% discount and VAT exemption on all public transit.

=== OPERATING HOURS ===
- Jeepneys: Generally 24/7, but most routes stop accepting passengers by 11:00 PM.
- LRT-1: 4:00 AM to 10:00 PM (Monday to Sunday).
- MRT-3: 4:30 AM to 9:30 PM (Monday to Sunday).

=== GENERAL RULES ===
- No eating or drinking inside jeepneys and trains.
- Face masks are no longer mandatory but highly recommended in crowded areas.
- Priority seats are strictly reserved for PWDs, Senior Citizens, and Pregnant Women.
